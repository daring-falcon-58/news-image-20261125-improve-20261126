## How it was built

A new layout, not a polish of round 2: a navy masthead band ("THE WORLD IN ONE
DAY"), six photo-led stories in two rows of three, and a 36-item single-line
brief in two columns, plus a markets strip and a DATE CHECK footer. All text
is drawn programmatically with PIL (DejaVu) — zero AI-rendered lettering, so
nothing can garble. The six photos are fresh generations made for this round;
the image is 1024×1024 JPEG, 373,153 bytes (limit 1 MiB). The composer asserts
every headline wraps to at most 2 lines, every sub to at most 2 lines, every
brief fits its column on one line, the masthead subline and markets strip fit
inside the canvas, and the layout ends inside 1024 px — it failed three times
during the build (briefs too long, layout 14 px over, subline/markets clipped
at the edges) and each was fixed by shortening copy and re-flowing, not by
shrinking type below legibility. Two runs of the composer produce identical
bytes (sha256
`496995e073bc2d132b37e56b51496b43b91d2dfa31a06bb5ac336e100013e830`), and
`node main.ts` reproduces the same bytes twice.
